## Data & Privacy Statement **Where data is stored:** Pokerly uses Forge-hosted storage (KVS) within the customer's Atlassian environment. The app does not operate external servers or make external data egress calls. **What is stored:** Pokerly stores app settings (including account IDs of session managers and observers, the default Jira field, and the selected Insights method), card templates, sessions (including names, descriptions, issue keys, issue summaries and icons for queued issues, and final estimates), votes per round (account ID and selected card), presence information (account ID, display name and avatar URL, refreshed hourly), and estimation history per session and issue for Insights. **Personal data:** Pokerly processes Atlassian account IDs and may temporarily cache display names and avatar URLs for session presence. The app does not store email addresses. **Retention:** Deleting a session removes its associated votes and presence data. Estimation history is retained for Insights until the app is uninstalled. Uninstalling Pokerly removes its Forge-hosted application data. **Access and permissions:** Jira API calls are performed as the signed-in user (`asUser`), so Jira permissions are respected. All data mutations are validated server-side against the user's Pokerly role. **Data sharing:** Pokerly does not sell or share customer data with third parties.
